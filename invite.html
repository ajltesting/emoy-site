EMOY is one live record of your child’s day, written by the people actually caring for them — and a way to share the parts worth keeping with family, without your child becoming a group chat.

Email us for an invite See how it works

That opens your mail app with a message ready to send. If it does not, write to support@emoy.app and say you would like an invite.

  • Two or more caregivers, one record. Start a feed on one phone and end it on the other. Fix a time someone else logged. Everyone writes, not just one person.
  • You choose what leaves the household. A grandparents circle might get photos and milestones but not the growth chart or the daily log. A friends circle might get photos only.
  • No ads, ever. Nothing counting how you use the app. No recordings of your screen. No trackers from other companies.

It is an iPhone app today, in private beta — not on the App Store or Google Play yet. We will send you what you need to get started.
